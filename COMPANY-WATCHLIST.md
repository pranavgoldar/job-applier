# Company watchlist

The catalog contains 111 employers and four remote-job searches. The 21 November update adds 29 fintech and consumer employers with a named opening on an employer careers page. Sixteen were verified in Bangalore, ten in Mumbai, two in Singapore and one in Dubai. Open **Companies → Evidence** for the role, city, source and date. The machine-readable records are in [the company catalog](src/target-companies.json).

With automatic YC reading off, 83 employers have a connected feed and 28 need a manual visit. The following tables document the earlier 16 November check. The catalog includes the later corrections to employer boards.

A fact is only recorded with a source. "Not verified" means no source was found, not that the answer is no.

- **YC:** confirmed against the Y Combinator company directory (23 companies).
- **India hiring:** counted from the company's own job board on the day checked.
- **Funding:** recorded only where a published source was read.

To add a company, use **Companies → Add company** in the dashboard, or add an entry to `src/target-companies.json` and raise `catalogVersion` in `src/watchlist.mjs`.
